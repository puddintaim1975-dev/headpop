## Full page description (markdown)

🎈 **i'm squishin your head!** 🎈

The world's most honest party game. No levels. No ads. No plot. Just:

1. Point the camera at a friend
2. Watch the balloon-head appear
3. **PINCH with two fingers**
4. *squelch* *squelch* **POP** 💥
5. Repeat until everyone has been squished

**The tech, honestly:** the camera feed stays on your phone. Face detection runs entirely on-device (ML Kit, bundled — works on de-Googled ROMs, no Play Services needed). The app has **no internet permission at all**. It cannot phone home, track you, or show you ads. It's a camera filter that commits assault.

**Features:**
- 🎈 Balloon-heads on every face the camera sees
- 🤏 Pinch-to-squish: the head compresses with your fingers, eyes bulge, pupils panic
- 🔊 Rubbery squish sounds that rise in pitch as you squeeze
- 💥 POP: particles, haptics, points
- ⚡ Combo multiplier — chain pops within 2 seconds for up to ×5
- 🤳 Front camera mode: squish your own head
- 🏆 Best score tracking
- 🚫 No ads, no tracking, no internet permission, nothing leaves your phone

**How to install:** download the APK, tap it, allow "install from unknown apps" when Android asks, squish. Android 7.0+. Camera permission required (that's the whole game).

**Tips:** this game is free and always will be. If someone's head-popping made you laugh, toss a quarter in the tip jar — 100% goes to buying the developer more gumballs.
